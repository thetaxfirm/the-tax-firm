## Idea 1: "Old Money Sophistication" — Neoclassical Finance Aesthetic

**Design Movement**: Inspired by private banking interfaces, old-money luxury, and editorial finance publications like Barron's and The Economist.

**Core Principles**:
1. Understated authority — every element whispers competence, not shouts
2. Editorial precision — content treated like a premium financial publication
3. Asymmetric elegance — layouts that feel curated, not templated
4. Tactile depth — surfaces that feel like embossed stationery

**Color Philosophy**: Deep charcoal (#1A1A2E) as the dominant tone, paired with antique gold (#C9A84C) for accents — evoking the gravitas of a private vault. Warm ivory (#FAF7F0) for content areas, creating contrast that feels like parchment against leather. Muted sage (#7A8B6F) as a secondary accent for trust and growth.

**Layout Paradigm**: Magazine-style editorial grid with intentional asymmetry. Hero section uses a dramatic split — text on one side with a tall, cropped portrait of Christopher Craig. Content sections alternate between full-bleed imagery and tightly constrained text columns. Generous negative space between sections creates breathing room.

**Signature Elements**:
1. Thin gold horizontal rules that divide sections like chapter breaks
2. Large, oversized serif numerals for statistics (reminiscent of financial reports)
3. Subtle paper texture overlay on light sections

**Interaction Philosophy**: Restrained and purposeful. Hover states reveal information like turning a page. Scroll-triggered reveals are slow and dignified — no bouncing or playful animations.

**Animation**: Fade-up reveals with 600ms easing. Parallax on hero imagery at 0.3x speed. Counter animations for statistics that count up slowly. Gold accent lines that draw themselves on scroll.

**Typography System**: Display: "Playfair Display" (serif) for headlines — authoritative and timeless. Body: "Source Sans 3" (sans-serif) for readability. Accent: "Cormorant Garamond" for pull quotes and testimonials. Strict hierarchy: H1 at 4rem, H2 at 2.5rem, body at 1.125rem.

</idea>

## Idea 2: "Architectural Brutalism Meets Finance" — Structural Confidence

**Design Movement**: Inspired by Swiss/International typography, Brutalist web design, and modern architecture firms like Zaha Hadid's website.

**Core Principles**:
1. Structural honesty — bold grids, exposed typography, raw confidence
2. Monumental scale — oversized type and dramatic whitespace
3. Geometric precision — sharp angles, clean intersections
4. High contrast — black and white with a single accent color

**Color Philosophy**: Pure black (#000000) and crisp white (#FFFFFF) as the foundation — no gray areas, literally. A single electric copper (#E87040) accent for CTAs and key moments. This creates maximum contrast and visual impact, suggesting clarity and decisiveness in tax strategy.

**Layout Paradigm**: Rigid 12-column grid with elements that intentionally break it. Full-width text blocks alternate with tightly constrained columns. Sections separated by bold horizontal lines. Content bleeds to edges. No rounded corners anywhere.

**Signature Elements**:
1. Oversized monospace numbers that span multiple columns
2. Diagonal slash motifs that cut through sections
3. Bold, all-caps section labels positioned as margin annotations

**Interaction Philosophy**: Direct and immediate. Click targets are large and obvious. Hover states use color inversion. No ambiguity in what's interactive.

**Animation**: Sharp, snappy transitions (200ms). Elements slide in from edges with hard stops. No easing curves — linear movement. Text reveals character by character for headlines.

**Typography System**: Display: "Space Grotesk" for headlines — geometric and modern. Body: "IBM Plex Sans" for content. Monospace: "JetBrains Mono" for numbers and data. H1 at 6rem (intentionally oversized), H2 at 3rem, body at 1rem.

</idea>
<text>A brutalist-meets-finance approach with monumental typography, maximum contrast, and structural honesty. Black, white, and electric copper create an atmosphere of decisive confidence.</text>

## Idea 3: "Midnight Boardroom" — Dark Luxury Advisory

**Design Movement**: Inspired by luxury hospitality brands (Aman Resorts, Four Seasons Private Residences), high-end fintech (Wealthsimple, Carta), and premium SaaS dashboards.

**Core Principles**:
1. Dark immersion — deep backgrounds that create focus and exclusivity
2. Luminous accents — warm gold and amber that glow against darkness
3. Cinematic storytelling — each scroll position tells part of the narrative
4. Spatial depth — layered cards, glass morphism, and subtle gradients

**Color Philosophy**: Rich midnight navy (#0B1120) as the primary background — darker than typical navy, suggesting depth and sophistication. Warm amber-gold (#D4A853) for primary accents and CTAs — the color of wealth and success. Soft platinum (#E8E4DD) for body text on dark backgrounds. Deep teal (#1B4D5C) as a secondary accent for variety. Sections alternate between dark immersive and warm ivory (#FDFAF5) for readability.

**Layout Paradigm**: Full-viewport cinematic sections that stack vertically. Hero takes 100vh with a dramatic gradient overlay on imagery. Service cards use a staggered masonry-like arrangement. The "About Christopher Craig" section uses a dramatic side-by-side with his photo taking 40% width. Process section uses a horizontal timeline that scrolls into view.

**Signature Elements**:
1. Subtle radial gradient glows behind key elements (like a spotlight effect)
2. Glass-morphism cards with frosted backgrounds and thin gold borders
3. Animated gradient mesh backgrounds on dark sections

**Interaction Philosophy**: Smooth and immersive. Elements emerge from darkness into light. Hover states add a warm glow. The experience feels like walking through an exclusive private club.

**Animation**: Smooth spring animations (800ms with slight overshoot). Staggered reveals where child elements animate in sequence. Parallax depth on layered elements. Subtle floating animation on accent elements. Counter animations with easing for statistics.

**Typography System**: Display: "DM Serif Display" for headlines — warm, authoritative serif with character. Body: "DM Sans" for content — clean and modern, pairs perfectly. Accent: "Libre Baskerville" for testimonial quotes. H1 at 4.5rem with tight letter-spacing (-0.02em), H2 at 2.75rem, body at 1.125rem with 1.7 line-height.

</idea>
